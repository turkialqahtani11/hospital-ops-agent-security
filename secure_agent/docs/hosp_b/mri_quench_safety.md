## Daily check
Record the helium level of the MagnaScan 3T (asset prefix MRI) every morning. Below 60 percent requires a vendor refill order.

## Quench response
The emergency quench button is used only when a person is trapped against the magnet by a ferromagnetic object
or in a fire. After any quench the magnet is out of service until the vendor re-ramps it.
