# SOP-A-GEN-03 Emergency Generator Monthly Load Test (SYNTHETIC)

Hospital A, Facilities Engineering. Synthetic training document.

## Frequency
Each emergency generator (asset prefix GEN) is load-tested monthly for at least 30 minutes.

## Acceptance criteria
- Transfer to emergency power within 10 seconds of utility failure.
- Load of at least 30 percent of nameplate rating during the test.
- Exhaust temperature and voltage within manufacturer limits.

## Records
Log start time, transfer time, load percentage and any alarms in the work order.
A failed transfer test is a CRITICAL event: open a priority CRITICAL work order immediately and notify the facilities manager.
