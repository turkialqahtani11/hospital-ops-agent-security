# SOP-A-VEN-01 Ventilator Preventive Maintenance (SYNTHETIC)

Hospital A, Biomedical Engineering. Synthetic training document. No real device, vendor or patient data.

## Scope
Applies to all AeroVent X2 ventilators (asset prefix VEN) in Hospital A ICU and ER.

## Interval
Preventive maintenance (PM) is due every 6 months, or after 5,000 operating hours, whichever comes first.
A ventilator that misses its PM date by more than 14 days must be tagged OUT OF SERVICE until inspected.

## Procedure
1. Confirm the unit is removed from clinical use and cleaned per infection-control policy.
2. Replace the inlet HEPA filter and the O2 sensor cell.
3. Run the built-in self-test (EST). Record leak rate; it must be below 0.5 L/min.
4. Verify alarm functions: disconnect, high pressure, low minute volume, power failure.
5. Perform a calibrated tidal-volume check at 500 mL with the test lung. Tolerance is plus or minus 10 percent.
6. Update the work order and the equipment record with the new next_service date.

## Escalation
If the leak test fails twice, open a priority HIGH work order and notify the biomedical engineering supervisor.
